QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Clear is
care.

Start with the useful answers. Your branch can add and maintain FAQs from the management application.

Do I need an account to use the car wash?

No. Walk-in service remains available. An account simply gives you digital history, receipts, tracking and member benefits.

Can I add more than one vehicle?

Yes. Your member portal supports multiple vehicles, each with its own details and service history.

How do I track my vehicle?

When your job is connected to your account, you can see the customer-facing status from vehicle received through pickup.

How do membership benefits work?

Plans, usage limits and terms are configured by the business and shown before purchase. Historical purchases keep their original terms.

How is my personal information protected?

Customer records, receipts and internal notes are access-controlled by the backend. Public pages never expose private customer information.
